- FORWARD 1 uses high-school standards placed at Grade 9 in this bank, and FORWARD 2 uses high-school standards placed at Grade 10. Each high-school code is used in only one of the two slots, so its placement is the same everywhere it appears. Seven FORWARD 2 branches are marked NEAREST RELATED (S3, S11, S33, S36, S38, S41, S47).
- These are checks of the exported bank. Grading of drawing, work, and construction responses has not been checked in the live game.

## Paths

- **Services, freelancing & local businesses** (`services`): Selling your time and skill: tutoring, cleaning, lawn care, repair, design, bookkeeping help. Path sets: 136, 137, 138, 139, 140. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Physical products & e-commerce** (`products`): Making or buying things and selling them in person or online, including shipping and returns. Path sets: 9, 17, 56, 95, 96, 105, 135, 141, 142, 143, 144, 145. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Digital products, software & AI-assisted businesses** (`digital`): Templates, courses, apps and online tools: access, support, updates and recurring costs. Path sets: 18, 146, 147, 148, 149, 150. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Content creation & social-media businesses** (`content`): Videos, posts, podcasts and newsletters earning from sponsors, affiliates, platforms or products. Path sets: 151, 152, 153, 154, 155, 156. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.

# PDF volumes: page and question ranges

The question collection (one question per page, with its teaching passage above the question and a reference label at the bottom) is split at the start of a set, about 500 pages per volume. Page numbers are pages of the full PDF (`Entrepreneurship_Question_Collection.pdf`; the `question_page` field of the answer key). Every page of every volume was compared with the same page of the full PDF when this index was written; all match.

The answer key holds several answers per page, so a volume boundary can share a page with the next volume; that page appears in both.

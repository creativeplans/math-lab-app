## Structure rules

- Puzzle sets: 27 of 54 (at least half required).
- Puzzle sets among the first 10: 6 (at least 6 required; set 1 is a puzzle).
- Longest run of formal sets: 2 (at most 2 allowed).

## Standards and practices

Two sets align directly to a Common Core standard, after checking the standard's wording against the questions:

- **HSA-REI.A.1** (set 39): "Explain each step in solving a simple equation as following from the equality of numbers asserted at the previous step, starting from the assumption that the original equation has a solution. Construct a viable argument to justify a solution method."
- **HSG-CO.C.9** (set 43): "Prove theorems about lines and angles. Theorems include: vertical angles are congruent; when a transversal crosses parallel lines, alternate interior angles are congruent and corresponding angles are congruent; points on a perpendicular bisector of a line segment are exactly those equidistant from the segment's endpoints."

Every other set is assessed against its local outcome (listed above) and names the Standards for Mathematical Practice it supports:

- MP1 Make sense of problems and persevere in solving them: supported by 19 sets.
- MP2 Reason abstractly and quantitatively: supported by 4 sets.
- MP3 Construct viable arguments and critique the reasoning of others: supported by 28 sets.
- MP6 Attend to precision: supported by 19 sets.
- MP7 Look for and make use of structure: supported by 22 sets.
- MP8 Look for and express regularity in repeated reasoning: supported by 5 sets.

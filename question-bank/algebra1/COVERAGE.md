## 7. Gaps and limitations

- **Standards list.** The 60 standards are the Algebra I course of the Common Core traditional pathway (CCSS Appendix A, Model Course Pathways), written as leaf codes. Appendix A could not be downloaded during authoring; the list was built from it as published and checked against district Algebra I maps. A reviewer should confirm it against the adopted Algebra I course before import.
- **Course placement of forward standards** (`engine/hs_courses.py`) follows Appendix A, with two choices of this bank: S-IC, S-CP, S-MD, and S-ID.A.4 are placed in Probability & Statistics, and every (+) standard is placed in Precalculus.
- **Complex numbers.** Appendix A leaves quadratics with complex solutions to Algebra II. MAIN questions answer "no real solution"; complex solutions appear only in forward branches (N-CN.C.7, N-CN.C.9).
- **A-REI.D.11** names linear, polynomial, rational, absolute value, exponential, and logarithmic functions. The MAIN questions use quadratic, linear, absolute value, and exponential functions; logarithmic and rational cases are left to the forward branches (Algebra II and Precalculus).
- **F-IF.C.7.b** step functions have one MAIN question (the greatest integer function); the other four cover absolute value, piecewise, square root, and cube root functions.
- **N-Q.A.1-3** are modeling standards that apply throughout the course. They have one focused set each, and many other questions state units and rounding.
- **No difficulty or hint fields.** As in Grades 3-8, difficulty is carried by the section (Section 3), and each question's explanation is in its `grading_note`, which can be shown as feedback after grading.
- **Answer checking.** The game's answer checker has not been seen. Questions marked "Any equivalent form" need a checker that can recognize equivalent expressions (for example 2.5 and 5/2, or 4(0.5)^x and 4(1/2)^x) or a person. Questions marked "Form required" accept only the stated form (factored, expanded, vertex form, exact, rounded).
- **Drawing and figures.** Graphing questions (`plot`, `plot_text`) need a drawing surface or teacher grading. The figure renderer draws solid lines only, so no figure shows a dashed boundary: inequality graphs are drawn by students, and one question describes a boundary line in words.
- **Technology.** Questions that ask for a correlation coefficient, logarithm values, or rounded roots expect a calculator (listed in Section 6).

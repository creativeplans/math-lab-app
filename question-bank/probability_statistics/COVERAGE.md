# Probability & Statistics coverage report

Generated by `probability_statistics/src/coverage_report.py` from the collection data: 54 sets, 1435 questions. Course placement labels (not grade levels) are used throughout.

## 1. Course profile

- **Course:** a high-school Probability & Statistics course (typically taken in grade 10, 11 or 12). No grade level is assigned; questions carry course-placement labels.
- **CORE strand:** every Common Core High School Statistics & Probability (HSS) standard that is not marked (+): 25 standards, with HSS-ID.B.6 and its lettered parts a, b and c listed separately.
- **ADVANCED EXTENSION strand:** every HSS standard marked (+): HSS-CP.B.8, HSS-CP.B.9 and all of HSS-MD (10 standards; HSS-MD.B.5.a and HSS-MD.B.5.b are separate). Each has its own MAIN set(s); strand = "advanced" in the export.
- **Distinct demands:** standards with several demands are split into subskills (section 3); every subskill is the focus of at least one MAIN set, so the collection has 54 sets for 35 standards.
- **Source caveat:** the official pages could not be opened from the authoring environment. Standard codes and wording were written from the published CCSS and must be spot-checked (README, Assumptions).

## 2. Overlap with other collections in this bank

The same HSS standards appear in other course collections, because the Common Core traditional pathway spreads statistics over Algebra I (S-ID), Geometry (S-CP) and Algebra II (S-ID.A.4, S-IC, S-MD.B.6-7). This collection is self-contained: it does not import, edit or depend on those files. When an S-ID code is used as a prerequisite (backward branch) it is labeled Algebra I; in MAIN and forward sections every HSS code is labeled Probability & Statistics (the 10 S-ID codes shared with Algebra I say "revisits an Algebra I standard").

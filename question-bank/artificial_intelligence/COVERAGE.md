## Paths

- **Everyday use** (`everyday`): Research, writing, study, planning, files and the practical pitfalls of AI tools. Path sets: 51, 54, 55, 57. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Creative work** (`creative`): Images, design, presentations, audio, music and video, and the rights that come with them. Path sets: 72, 73, 74, 79, 81, 82. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Coding & automation** (`coding`): Reading and checking code, tests, security, agents and automated workflows. Path sets: 85, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Work & careers** (`work`): AI in jobs, AI-related roles, choosing and paying for tools, and measuring results. Path sets: 101, 103, 104, 105, 107. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
- **Society, law & philosophy** (`society`): Fairness, law and policy, responsibility, philosophy of mind, history and future claims. Path sets: 124, 140, 141, 142, 143, 147, 148, 149, 150, 153. Learners on this path also take every foundation set.
